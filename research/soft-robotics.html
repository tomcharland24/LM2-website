      Soft Robotics

      Vision

      Soft robots use compliant materials and large deformation to create movement. We study how a mechanism’s shape, material and actuation determine where it bends, how it makes contact and how much force it can exert.

      We combine modelling with prototypes and measurements to design movements that remain repeatable even when the mechanism touches an object. For example, a gripper can use its geometry and material compliance to wrap around objects of different sizes.

      Why it matters

      Compliance helps distribute contact forces and accommodate variation in the environment. It also means that the robot’s shape and motion depend on the forces it encounters. Predicting these interactions is important for tasks that require both a gentle touch and reliable handling.

      Designing motion and sensing together can help a flexible device adjust to changing conditions and provide feedback about its own deformation.

      Potential impact

      This research could support gentle handling of fragile objects, movement in confined spaces and devices that combine sensing with compliant actuation.

      • Gentle grasping
      • Adaptive motion in confined spaces
      • Integrated sensing in flexible devices
