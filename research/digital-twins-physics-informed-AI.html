Research theme

      Digital Twins & Physics-Informed AI

      Vision

      A digital twin uses observations from a physical system to update a model of its behaviour. We study how to combine mechanics, sensor measurements and machine learning so that the model can follow changes during operation.

      One research direction is to embed the equations of motion or material behaviour into the learning process. Our aim is to use available data to estimate quantities that are hard to measure directly, such as loads or states inside a structure.

      Why it matters

      Experiments and industrial systems rarely provide measurements at every point or under every operating condition. A model informed by physics can help interpret sparse signals and identify where their explanation remains uncertain.

      An important part of the work is checking predictions against conditions that were unavailable during model development. This helps establish whether a change in the measurements represents a real change in the system.

      Potential impact

      Reliable estimates could support process monitoring, help identify changes earlier and inform decisions about where further measurements or inspections are needed.

      • Earlier detection of changes
      • Faster model updates
      • Better-informed inspection planning
