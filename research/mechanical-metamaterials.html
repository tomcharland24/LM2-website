Research theme

      Mechanical Metamaterials

      Smart structures use geometry, materials, and mechanics to change shape, function, or response.

      Vision

      This theme explores architected structures, compliant mechanisms, deployable systems, and responsive devices. Projects focus on useful motion, controlled deformation, and design principles that scale.

      LM2 uses ideas from kirigami, soft robotics, mechanics, and fabrication to create structures that can deploy, morph, absorb energy, or interact with their environment. The work is visual, physical, and strongly connected to applications.

      Why it matters

      Adaptive structures can reduce weight, simplify mechanisms, and create new possibilities in robotics, aerospace, and biomedical engineering.

      Potential impact

      • Lightweight structures that deploy or morph on demand.
      • Mechanisms with fewer rigid parts and simpler actuation.
      • Design rules for future adaptive engineering systems.
