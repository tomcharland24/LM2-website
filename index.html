      LM2 develops predictive tools for material behavior and applies them to micro-manufacturing, smart structures, and multiscale engineering challenges.

      About LM2

      A lab connecting materials, models, and manufacturing.

      Our work combines experiments, numerical modeling, and fabrication to understand how materials behave across scales and how this knowledge can support useful engineering systems.

      Team

      A multidisciplinary and international group.

      The LM2 team brings together five professors with complementary expertise, postdoctoral fellows, PhD students, master's students, and collaborators. Members come from many parts of the world, which gives the lab a broad scientific culture and a strong mix of perspectives.
