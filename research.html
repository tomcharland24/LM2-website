Research

      Research themes

      LM2 research connects mechanics, materials, fabrication, and application-driven design across multiple scales.

      Multiscale Mechanics

      Connect local mechanisms such as strain localization and crack growth to the response of components through experiments and modelling across scales.

      Mechanical Metamaterials

      Design repeating architectures whose geometry governs how they deform, transmit loads and absorb energy.

      Multistable Structures

      Design structures that can switch between stable shapes through elastic instabilities and retain each shape without continuous actuation.

      Biomimetics & Bio-Inspired Systems

      Identify how living systems use geometry, material organization and growth to achieve mechanical functions.

      Deployable Structures Inspired by Origami and Kirigami

      Use folds and cuts to program how flat sheets deploy into functional three-dimensional structures.

      Soft Robotics

      Design compliant actuators and mechanisms that use large, controlled deformation to interact with their surroundings.

      Fluid-Structure Interactions

      Study how flows deform flexible structures and how that deformation changes the surrounding flow.

      Additive Manufacturing

      Develop printing processes that control the geometry and internal structure of components during deposition.

      Additive Manufacturing of Smart Composites

      Integrate sensing and actuation directly into additively manufactured composite parts.

      Digital Twins & Physics-Informed AI

      Combine physical models, sensor data and machine learning to estimate the changing state of structures and processes.
